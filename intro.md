$\delta$				: delta, 4th letter in Greek's alphabet, used as the symbol of change.

column vector	:**`[1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6]`**

⊕ 				: sum operator, denotes concatenation

$|x|$				: absolute value, denotes the modulus (the positive part) of a scalar $x$

det **A**			: determinant of a Matrix **A**

$x \sim p(x)$		: signifies that $x$ is sampled from the distribution $p(x)$

$var[f(x)]$		: variance of $f(x)$

$\forall$				: for all, so $\forall m \in M$ means all the value of $m$ within the set of $M$. 

$\mathbb{R}$				: denotes real number.
